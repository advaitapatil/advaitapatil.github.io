I'm a medical student at [Harvard Medical School](https://hms.harvard.edu/) and *Head of Health and Life Sciences* at [Quadrillion Labs](http://quadrillion.io). I previously received a master's in Computer Science (AI track) and bachelor’s degrees in Computer Science and Biology from [Stanford](https://cs.stanford.edu/).

I research the integration of human and artificial intelligence in neurosurgery. I lead initiatives at [Mass General Brigham](https://www.massgeneral.org/neurosurgery) translating machine learning advances into clinically-deployed algorithms and rigorous studies to improve safety, access, and outcomes in neurosurgical care.

I’ve spent time innovating from molecular to population scales at [Stanford ChEM-H](https://chemh.stanford.edu/) & [Bioengineering](https://bioengineering.stanford.edu/), [NASA Astrobiology](https://www.nasa.gov/space-science-and-astrobiology-at-ames/), [Stanford Health Care](https://med.stanford.edu/gensurg.html), the [Center for International Security and Cooperation](https://cisac.fsi.stanford.edu/), [MIT](https://cheme.mit.edu/), and [UCSF](https://bts.ucsf.edu/). In a former life I was [invited to The White House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Scholars_Program), was a [Science Talent Search](https://www.societyforscience.org/regeneron-sts/) finalist, and attended a (wonderful) [nerd camp](https://www.cee.org/programs/research-science-institute) at MIT. You can find my [main-belt asteroid](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/tools/sbdb_lookup.html#/?sstr=34256) dancing around somewhere between Mars and Jupyter.

When not at the bench, computer, or hospital, I enjoy long bike rides and reflecting on our world's natural wonders through [nature photography and poetry](http://naturephotopoetry.wordpress.com).
